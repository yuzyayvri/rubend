recording defect, not a solver failure or rejected performance treatment.

Its control profile then hits the fixed ninety-second sample deadline after
4,000 of 4,096 requested entries. The producer terminates normally by SIGTERM,
and the exact native and detached-worker identities are gone. The outer resource
receipt has no resource stop. No candidate profile or scored batch starts.

successful preparation data, partial output and failed sources are preserved in

historical flag cannot support a later direct filesystem recheck. Do not invent
the missing path or relabel the incomplete profile passed.

diagnostic. The recovery separates mode-specific workloads. Profiling uses
128 repeats and the original ninety-second cap. Scored runs retain 512 repeats,
all eight cases, forty seconds per child, the one-second CPU floor and the
unchanged five-percent duplicate and ten-percent numerical gates. The focused
regression fails before the correction with `512 !== 128` and passes afterward.

check the existing scored workload's time/quantization admission, not select a
ratio or winner. A failed projection gate produces an explicitly unmeasured
disposition. No scoring threshold is relaxed. New samples record the owned IPC
directory, and the controller directly checks its absence and native PID/start
identity after every sample and again at confirmation.
