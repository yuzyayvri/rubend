## Disposition

The [canonical archive](u06-render-retain-v1/index.json) verifies 2,171 source
seals, three receipts, all fifteen partial records, worker termination and no
resource stop. The hidden page remains document-visible. All 870 pose audits,
513 canonical-color checks, 315 repaint checks and fifteen recovery cycles pass.
All samples preserve zero idle draws. Retained sets create zero meshes/groups
after initialization, versus 80/26 in both old-code controls. Candidate normal
repaint latency peaks at about 18.2 ms. The temporary tab is closed.

Timing acceptance is inconclusive. Three of five A/A cohorts fail the frozen
active p95 floor, with a maximum symmetric spread of 16.37 percent. The candidate
also misses the cadence floor in some pairs. No causal regression or speedup can
be established under those controls. Render submission duration does not measure
GPU completion or explain compositor/RAF/GC delays. Their contributions remain
unprofiled. Do not rerun the same cohorts until a favorable result appears.

Keep retention private and unpromoted. The accepted application remains unchanged.
This finishes the current retention screen with a structural win and inconclusive
cadence disposition, not a performance-accepted integration. Continue U04's
phase-two projection grounding and bounded pilot; existing law/build authority
gates still apply. Operator review/video/package gates remain open for U06.
