# Frozen single-seed quality comparison

The [native guard](u04-seed-bank-v1/index.json) passed before this screen.
No solver code changes here. Reuse its exact native executable only while its
binary digest, build record and complete source closure remain unchanged.
The small tier and product remain untouched.

The hypothesis is that a different one-move starting point finds a shorter
incumbent at the same total search work. Freeze three separate configurations,
U, R and F quarter-turn preturns. Each owns one seeded Fast bank, receives one
quantum after seven original quanta, and shares one owned table. These are not
three simultaneous banks. Seeded work cannot raise the original lower bound.
The disabled seed is the unchanged round-robin control. No rejected ordering
treatment, cache, bound or quantum tuning is combined with this experiment.

`bench/seed-bank-quality-spec.json` fixes all choices before data collection.
First, validate all three seeds on the native pilot/public roots. Disabled
states must equal the saved control and U states must equal the previous guard.
The shared result model is checked against that saved guard and eight actual
wire corruptions, including invalid completion codes and child-bound leakage.

Use four pilot roots plus public Superflip, fast/exact requests and independent
160,000- and 640,000-step budgets. Two control A/A cohorts precede five cohorts
alternating all four configurations. Every bank's work is charged. A final
quantum may be shortened to end exactly at the shared allowance. Repeated
cohorts are timing repetitions of twenty distinct case/request/budget pairs,
not one hundred independent cube samples. All repeated fixed-work results
must be deterministic.

A missing answer, longer answer, lower established bound or lost optimum rejects
an arm, even if another field improves. Among arms with no loss and at least one
distinct gain, rank coverage gains first, then HTM saved among jointly answered
cases, new completed optima, lower-bound gain and smaller seed ID. This choice
is executable, not a later judgment call. Ties retain the control. Missing or
nonrepeatable data cannot select a winner. Retain every arm's raw results.
Optimal means the existing search-established status, not formal certification.

Only a selected no-loss arm opens disjoint development confirmation, cases
512–519, using the same budgets and five alternating cohorts. These differ
from the earlier ordering confirmation's cases 0–7. The release holdout is not
used. Failed confirmation keeps the control. Do not retune allocation or rerun
unchanged cohorts until a favorable result appears.

Report answer quality at fixed search-advance work, not at a wall deadline.
Native compute intervals include initial bank setup, certificates, search and
sharing. They exclude resident-table loading, output serialization and transport.
These are elapsed work intervals, not process CPU measurements.
Save five-run medians/ranges and A/A spread, but make no latency promotion.
The coarse timer, missing dedicated search profile and missing application
deadline comparison keep performance acceptance false. A fixed-work gain only
justifies the next timed/integration experiment.

The queue `u04-seed-quality-v1` is expected to take five minutes, capped at
twenty-five minutes. Preparation uses two GiB. Native jobs use three GiB on
one low-priority lane, serially. No compile overlaps measurement. Confirmation
reuses passing pure-module evidence and reruns unchanged `just check`. Source,
binary, cache, commands and receipts are sealed. Independent review, new laws,
large builds, publishing and default promotion remain outside this screen.

The [completed archive](u04-seed-quality-v1/index.json) verifies 1,990 seals,
five receipts, clean worker exit and no resource stop. The pilot replays 9,078
original candidates and 1,840 child incumbents across 14,520 snapshots and
176,000,000 work steps, including repetitions. U and F have zero distinct wins
and two losses each. R has one distinct win and the same two losses. Every arm
therefore fails the frozen floor. No seed was selected and development was
skipped. Nineteen of twenty A/A pairs exceed five-percent spread. No timing,
strength or default promotion follows.

Both losses are exact-mode progress. Pilot1 at 160,000 steps keeps length20 but
loses lower bound13 to0. Pilot2 at640,000 keeps length20 but loses14 to13.
R's single win is fast-mode pilot1 at640,000, length20 to19. These repeat
identically in all five cohorts. Five seeded admissions are one distinct case,
not five independent wins. The unchanged original engine remains selected.
