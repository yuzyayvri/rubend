## What an observed trace span means

Match raw entry and exhaustion events by their full recorded cube and context.
For a completed entry, its observed interval is from its entry step through
its exhaustion step inclusive. For an unfinished entry, clip the observed
interval at the fixed work limit and mark it right-censored. Union intervals
within each root so a child and its ancestor cannot double-count work.
Retain completed, censored and unavailable-span counts separately.

These unions describe the unchanged recorded traversal only. They are not a
new solver run, actual saved search work, a cache benefit, CPU savings or an
upper bound on every unseen continuation. Small-Light snapshots have no matched
exhaustion events and therefore have unavailable spans, not invented zero cost.
The model tests overlapping intervals, censoring, strict threshold equality,
no-range and zero-signal outcomes, twelve invalid records and an unsafe-sum
counterexample.
