interior subset. Full keys include all five packed cube words, three cached
forward coordinates, radius, previous face, path depth, contour and incumbent
length. Root/session, profile, cache digest and search-policy digest scope each
analysis. Different previous faces or cached coordinates cannot share a witness.

An exhaustion needs its matching entry. A later entry can reference only an
earlier fully observed matching exhaustion. Budget-ended open contexts remain
censored. Nested work spans overlap, so their sum is not CPU savings. These are
observations under the current enumerator, not formal `CompletedFailure` proofs.
No witness is reused by the engine and NP06/NP07 remain unapproved.

root-mask contexts, zero radii/depths, wrong contour arithmetic, truncated

structural validity sampling, not independent replay of every interior path.

unchanged passed kernel evidence is reused, while unchanged `just check` runs.
No cache, database build, new law, performance promotion or publication occurs.

## Result

The [canonical archive](u05-interior-v1/index.json) verifies 2,026 source seals,
three resource receipts, worker termination and no resource stop. All 48
checkpoint incumbents replay independently. Every control/observer metadata and
stack pair matches. Native packet calibration and 2,048 sampled cube roundtrips
pass. The unchanged proof and toolchain checks pass.

The four roots produce 52,584 entries and 52,567 completed spans, with 17 open
contexts censored at the budget. One completed context repeats on pilot 3 in
contour 14. Its repeated span contains 19 advance calls. The other three roots
have no repeated completed context in this eligible subset.

This does not justify a native cache probe on the current workload. Every lookup
would carry a full-context cost to find that one short repeated span. This is a
scoped negative opportunity screen, not a proof that deeper search cannot benefit
from caching. No cache, pruning, partitioning or formal failure witness exists.
NP06/NP07 and independent review remain gated. Continue the U04 deadline probe.
