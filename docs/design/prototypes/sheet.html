Controls & guarantees

Position
Drag to orbit. Click a face, or use U R F D L B. Shift reverses a turn; Alt makes a half-turn. The camera never changes the canonical frame.
Playback
Space plays or pauses. Arrow keys step. The slider reconstructs the committed cube from checked replay.
Search
Fast mode improves a verified answer. Exact mode also searches shorter lengths. A timeout does not establish optimality.
Guarantees
Every accepted solution is replay-checked. A search-optimal result means the lower bound meets the verified length. The full search engine is not formally certified.
